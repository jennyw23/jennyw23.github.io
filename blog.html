What have I been working on?

If the pandemic has taught me anything, it’s to appreciate every social interaction. This sounds cheesy, but I genuinely mean it. Most of my personal project ideas have come from thinking about inefficiencies in society (how can we crowdsource office temperatures to reduce our carbon footprint?) or issues I’d want to solve in my own life (how do we eliminate food waste in Wellesley’s all-you-can-eat dining halls)

What’s been on my mind lately?

This past semester, I’ve had the chance to interact with so many incredible people. I truly feel that everyone I’ve spoken with––whether it be another student, a professor, or an old friend––has taught me something new. I’m also incredibly grateful to have enjoyed each and every one of my classes. I hope this blog serves as a sounding board for my thoughts about technology, diversity, and life in general.

And Who am I?

I’m currently a sophomore at Wellesley College, and I’ve spent my whole life battling New England winters. I love to travel, cook new recipes, and stay active. I’m also an avid coffee drinker. This summer, I will be interning at Lyft as a SWE Intern on the mobile development team. While working remotely from California, I hope to keep this site updated with my new experiences on the West Coast!
