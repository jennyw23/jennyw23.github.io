Culture Meets the Commute: Reclaiming Mobility with the Matatu

I've always been a huge fan of public transportation. I grew up in the suburbs, where a driver's license was the key to "freedom". On day trips to New York City or Boston, I'd frequently feel a rush of independence when riding the subways. In this audio project, I discuss innovations in the Global South's public transit systems and reflect on how the U.S. could learn from other cultures.

This post is an informal discussion I wrote in Spring 2021 for an MIT seminar called 21A.511: “Hacking from the South”. During the first half of the semester, we focused on the books Coding Freedom by Gabriella Coleman and Hacking Diversity by Christina Dunbar-Hester. After finishing the publications, both authors came to speak with the class about their experiences, research, and reflections since publishing their work.
