    California

Spontaneously, a few of my friends decided to visit Portland and Seattle before I returned to the east coast. I was able to meet my Lyft internship team (based in Seattle) and explore the Pacific Northwest's beautiful sunsets. There was something cathartic about being able to pick up and go anywhere. Some of my trip highlights include: exploring nighttime Chicago on the Fourth of July, accidentally hiking through a ghost town in Utah, and eating a 13-course meal at a Michelin-starred restaurant in San Francisco. This road trip surpassed any and all my expectations.

Again, my words cannot do it justice. I chronicle the trip in this Google Photos album.
