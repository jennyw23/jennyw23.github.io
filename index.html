What have I been working on?

If the pandemic has taught me anything, it’s to appreciate every social interaction. This sounds cheesy, but I genuinely mean it. Most of my personal project ideas have come from thinking about inefficiencies in society (how can we crowdsource office temperatures to reduce our carbon footprint?) or issues I’d want to solve in my own life (how do we eliminate food waste in Wellesley’s all-you-can-eat dining halls)

What’s been on my mind lately?

This past semester, I’ve had the chance to interact with so many incredible people. I truly feel that everyone I’ve spoken with––whether it be another student, a professor, or an old friend––has taught me something new.
